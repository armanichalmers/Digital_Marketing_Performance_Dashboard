# 📊 Digital Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **digital and social media marketing campaign performance** across 2023–2024. The interactive Power BI dashboard provides a consolidated view of advertising spend, revenue, return on ad spend (ROAS), conversions, click-through rate (CTR), audience performance, and channel efficiency across multiple digital advertising platforms.

The dashboard was designed to evaluate **cross-channel and social media campaign performance**, identify high-performing advertising channels and target audiences, measure marketing efficiency, and explore revenue and spending trends over time.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard was developed to analyze digital and social media advertising performance and answer key marketing questions, including:

- How much was spent across digital advertising campaigns?
- How much revenue was generated from marketing activity?
- What was the overall return on ad spend?
- Which advertising channels generated the strongest ROAS?
- How do revenue and advertising spend compare across channels?
- How have revenue and spend changed over time?
- Which target audiences generated the most conversions?
- Which channels were the most cost-efficient at generating conversions?

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights five primary marketing KPIs:

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| **Total Ad Spend** | **$14.0M** |
| **Total Revenue** | **$38.5M** |
| **ROAS** | **2.76x** |
| **Conversions** | **320K** |
| **CTR** | **2.55%** |

These KPIs provide an at-a-glance view of overall campaign investment, financial return, conversion activity, and engagement.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### ROAS by Channel

Compares return on ad spend across advertising channels, allowing high- and low-performing platforms to be identified quickly. Conditional color formatting provides a visual progression based on ROAS performance.

### Revenue vs. Spend by Channel

Compares total advertising investment with the revenue generated by each channel, providing context for differences in channel-level return.

### Revenue & Ad Spend Over Time

Tracks monthly revenue and advertising spend across 2023–2024 to highlight changes in campaign investment and financial performance over time.

### Conversions by Target Audience

Uses a donut chart to show the distribution of total conversions across audience age groups:

- 18–24
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- 55+
- All Ages

### Cost per Conversion by Channel

Compares the cost required to generate conversions across advertising channels, helping identify differences in acquisition efficiency.

## 🧮 DAX Measures

Custom DAX measures were created where aggregation required calculation within the current filter context.

### ROAS

```DAX
ROAS =
DIVIDE(
    SUM('marketing_campaigns'[Revenue]),
    SUM('marketing_campaigns'[Spend])
)
```

This calculates return on ad spend as total revenue divided by total advertising spend.

### Cost per Conversion

```DAX
Cost per Conversion =
DIVIDE(
    SUM('marketing_campaigns'[Spend]),
    SUM('marketing_campaigns'[Conversions])
)
```

This calculates advertising spend per conversion and dynamically recalculates as dashboard filters are applied.

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- The campaigns generated approximately **$38.5M in revenue from $14.0M in advertising spend**.
- Overall **ROAS was 2.76x**, indicating that the campaigns generated approximately $2.76 in revenue for every $1 spent on advertising.
- Channel-level ROAS analysis reveals differences in return across advertising platforms.
- Conversion distribution varies across target audience segments.
- Cost-per-conversion analysis provides an additional perspective on channel efficiency beyond ROAS alone.
- Interactive filters allow performance to be analyzed at the channel, campaign, audience, and date levels.
